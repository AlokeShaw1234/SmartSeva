Solutions Provided by SmartSeva

Efficient Civic Issue Reporting

Citizens can submit complaints instantly with photos, videos, and location details. Reduces delays for potholes, broken streetlights, or overflowing trash bins.

Real-Time Tracking

Track complaint status from submission to resolution, ensuring transparency and accountability in government response.

Interactive Mapping

Live maps with color-coded markers show reported issues, helping citizens and authorities identify problem hotspots efficiently.

Multilingual Access

Supports English, Hindi, Bengali, and regional languages, allowing citizens from all backgrounds to report and access services easily.

Mobile & Web Accessibility

Citizen-Government Collaboration

About Us – SmartSeva

SmartSeva is a citizen-centric platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local government authorities. Our mission is to make civic services transparent, responsive, and efficient.

With real-time issue reporting, interactive maps, and multilingual support, SmartSeva empowers citizens to actively participate in building smarter, cleaner, and safer cities.

On the administrative side, SmartSeva provides municipal staff and authorities with a centralized dashboard that streamlines complaint management, prioritizes issues, and enhances government accountability.

Our Vision: To create smarter cities where citizens’ voices are heard, and civic issues are resolved quickly and efficiently.

Our Mission: To empower citizens with an easy-to-use platform for reporting issues while providing governments with tools to improve responsiveness, transparency, and service quality.
